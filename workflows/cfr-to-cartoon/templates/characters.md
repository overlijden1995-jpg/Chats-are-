# Cast Bible — Recurring Characters

Fictional functionaries only. No real person is ever depicted, voiced, or
impersonated. Characters are built from CSS/SVG geometry and typography.

## The Anchor — *"Good evening. The war is on schedule."*

- **Visual:** a telescreen rectangle (bezel + scanlines) containing only a
  giant condensed headline and a `LIVE` bug. No face. Occasionally the
  screen shows `PLEASE STAND BY`.
- **Voice:** Piper, low & flat; ≤14-word sentences; never exclaims.
- **Function:** delivers official euphemisms with total sincerity.
- **Never:** winks at the audience, admits anything, changes expression
  (it has none — that's the point).

## The Clerk — *"Form 27B/6. In triplicate."*

- **Visual:** disembodied gloved hands (simple geometric shapes), a desk,
  stacked forms, and the Stamp. Brass form numbers float above each form.
- **Voice:** Piper, clipped & higher; speaks in form numbers and verdicts.
- **Function:** processes catastrophe as paperwork; stamps the verdict
  (`APPROVED` / `DENIED` / `PEACE`) that ends each gag sequence.
- **Never:** hurries, empathises, or stamps anything twice.

## The Map — *"The border has been improved."*

- **Visual:** abstract polygon territories in `--telescreen`, borders as
  dashed bone lines that creep when unobserved; labels are ministry
  euphemisms (`ZONE OF STABILITY`, `FORMERLY ZONE OF STABILITY`).
- **Voice:** none. The Map does not speak. Its labels change instead.
- **Function:** visualises territorial claims vs. the ledger's facts,
  which arrive as mono captions beneath it.

## The Ledger — the counting room (a place, not a person)

- **Visual:** full-frame mono table; numbers arrive one row at a time with
  a pneumatic-tube hiss; each row carries its `F-###` tag and `as_of`.
- **Voice:** The Anchor's voice, even flatter. Or silence with captions.
- **Function:** reads the episode's real statistics. Played straight.
  This room is the conscience of the show: no gag lands inside it.

## The Chorus of Communiqués

- **Visual:** three identical headline cards, stacked like choir stalls,
  each contradicting the previous — built only from documented `claim`
  ledger entries.
- **Voice:** all three voices in unison, if used at all.
- **Function:** official statements cancelling each other out in real time.

## Casting prohibitions

- No character may be named after, modelled on, or voiced to imitate a
  real official, leader, journalist, or victim.
- Documented real words appear only as attributed on-screen quotations.
- New characters require a bible entry here *before* they appear on screen.
